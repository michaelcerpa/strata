A worked example

Turning messy messages into clean records.

Strata reads a message and fills in a record. It scores how sure it is about each field and sends only the doubtful ones to a person. The same engine does both jobs below. The work it's based on is real. I learned it from two operators with 35+ years inside a national installer, and from three summers working in its branches. The project is imagined. No client hired me, and the demo plays back pre-written results, not live AI.

From a message to a record

An email, voicemail or text becomes one record

Pick a message. Clean ones go straight into the job system. Messy ones stop, with the weak fields marked for a scheduler. The customer never sees any of this.

From paper to records

An acquired company's paper files, ready for the integration team by morning

The files are scanned overnight and read by an AI model that can read images. Clean records are written automatically. Unclear or conflicting ones wait for a person. When the typed text and a handwritten note disagree, it shows both and doesn't pick.
